# POBLATION COMMUNITY & MODDING POLICY
Version 1.0
Last Updated: May 2026

---

# Philosophy

POBLATION is designed to be explored, modified, analyzed, and experimented with.

The project encourages:
- modding
- weird experiments
- fan systems
- procedural chaos
- AI behavior research
- community tools
- fan lore
- accessibility patches
- launcher themes
- gameplay transformations

The goal is not to lock the game away.
The goal is to prevent exploitation, impersonation, malware distribution, and ecosystem fragmentation.

---

# Allowed Community Content

The following are generally allowed:

- mods
- texture replacements
- launcher themes
- accessibility improvements
- gameplay rebalances
- localization projects
- fan translations
- procedural experiments
- UI overhauls
- challenge builds
- community patches
- custom template packs
- fan-made scenarios
- save analyzers
- external editors
- replay systems
- statistics tools
- lore analysis
- fan websites
- noncommercial videos and streams

---

# Patreon & Donations

Community creators MAY:
- accept donations
- run Patreon pages
- receive voluntary support
- accept Ko-fi contributions

ONLY if:
- supporters are funding the creator directly
- supporters are not paying for the game itself
- supporters are not paying for pirated builds
- supporters are not receiving commercial ownership rights
- supporters are not purchasing access to the official game

Allowed examples:
- early mod previews
- devlogs
- cosmetic community perks
- credits/supporter roles
- behind-the-scenes content

NOT allowed:
- selling the game
- paywalling official builds
- charging for access to official releases
- monetizing stolen launcher builds

---

# Fan Builds

Fan builds must:
- clearly identify themselves as unofficial
- avoid misleading branding
- avoid pretending to be official
- link back to the original project
- avoid malware or hidden software
- avoid commercial exploitation

Fan builds SHOULD:
- be transformative
- experiment creatively
- document changes
- preserve credits

---

# Launcher Policy

Only official launchers may:
- present themselves as the primary official launcher
- distribute official signed builds
- use official launcher branding
- access future official trust systems

Community launchers are allowed ONLY if:
- clearly unofficial
- noncommercial
- not misleading
- not designed to replace the official ecosystem

---

# Modding Philosophy

If someone turns POBLATION into:
- a political simulator
- a fantasy kingdom simulator
- a comedy disaster simulator
- a hyper-serious psychological experiment
- a terminal MMO fever dream

...good.

That means the systems are alive.

---

# Content Rules

Community content may NOT include:
- malware
- spyware
- hidden telemetry
- illegal content
- deceptive monetization
- impersonation of official staff
- fake security warnings
- fake launcher trust prompts

---

# Repository Mirrors

Unofficial mirrors are discouraged.

Approved archival mirrors must:
- preserve license files
- preserve credits
- preserve repository history
- avoid deceptive presentation

---

# Final Message

The community is part of the experiment.

Please do not turn the apocalypse into adware.
